📐 四类题型模板（✅必说 ➕选说）
📊 数据图
必说This image shows information about【主题】.
必说To begin with, the highest / largest value is【X】, at about【数字】.
必说In contrast, the lowest / smallest value is【Z】, at about【数字】.
选说Besides, the second highest is【Y】, at about【数字】.
必说Meanwhile, there is an increasing / decreasing trend from【起点】to【终点】.
选说However, some figures remained stable at【数字】.
必说In conclusion, this graph clearly shows【主题】.
必踩词：highest/largest · lowest/smallest · increase/decrease trend · percentage · in contrast
🔄 流程图
必说This image shows the procedure / process of【主题】.
必说There are【N】steps / stages in total.
必说To start with, the first step is【A】.
必说Then / Next, the second step goes into【B】.
必说After that,【C】.
必说Finally, the last step is【产出】.
选说However, if【条件】, then【另一结果】.
必踩词：procedure/steps · first step · second step · goes into · finally · produces
🗺️ 地图
必说This image shows a map of【主题】.
必说In the north / south / east / west / center of the map,【内容】.
必说In contrast,【另一区域内容】.
必说Besides,【A】was replaced by / was built / disappeared / expanded.
选说However, some parts remained unchanged.
必说Overall, this map shows【主题】.
必踩词：north/south/east/west/center · country/area/lake/ocean/mountain · replaced by · in contrast
🖼️ 图片图
必说This image shows【主题】.
必说In the top / bottom area, left / right side of the picture, we can see【内容】.
必说In the center of the picture, there is / are【数量】【人物/物品】.
选说Besides,【人物/物品】is doing / wearing / holding【细节】.
必说In contrast,【另一侧内容】.
选说Meanwhile, in the background / foreground,【内容】.
必说Overall, this picture clearly shows【主题】.
必踩词：top/bottom area · left/right side · center of the picture · in contrast · there is/are

📋 做题方法（注意事项 + 找关键句 + 改写 + 连接词）
⚠️ 注意事项
5-75 词（最佳 60-70）· 10 分钟（读 3-4 → 写 4-5 → 查 1-2）· 必须一个句子
🔍 找关键句
首尾句 / 转折词后（However 等）/ 因果句 / 强调句 / 重复主题词 / 例前主旨
✂️ 改写压缩
提取主谓宾 · 删破折号解释 / 引用语 / 同位语 · 合并列举 · 保留主题词+数字
🔗 连接词 + 替换
and/but/because/although/which… 一句到底；简单词简单替换防照抄
📐 万能四段式（✅背熟即用 · 4 句关键句 → 1 句 · 保证 60-70 词）
✍️ 第 1 部分 · 四段式骨架（每段放 1 个关键句）
段 1Although【关键句 1（主旨）】,
段 2【关键句 2（转折/因果）】,
段 3and【关键句 3（并列补充）】,
段 4which leads to【关键句 4（结论/结果）】.
变体Because【原因】,【结果】, and【补充点】　/　While【对比 A】,【对比 B】, since【依据】
🧭 第 2 部分 · 实战决策顺序（4 句并列时照做）
① 定主句选信息量最大 / 最概括的那句（通常是首尾句）作段 1。
② 问关系其余 3 句逐个问「它和主句是什么关系」：因果 because · 补充 which · 伴随 -ing · 附加 with · 对比 while。
③ 兜底真没法降级的 2 句并列核心事实 → 中间用 , and 连接。
④ 自查主句 = 1 个 · 句号 = 1 个 · 逗号后每段都不是完整句子 → 通过。
📊 第 3 部分 · 真题实战
Human remains are a fundamental part of the archaeological record, which pose distinctive ethical questions, representing traces of colonial exploitation, with the British government now curbing the study of excavated remains.
解读1 个主句 + which 从句 + -ing 分词 + with 短语 → 4 句并列合成 1 句。
必踩：单句 60-70 词 · 首字母大写 · 覆盖 4 个关键句 · 简单替换不照抄 · 结尾句号

📋 做题方法（三步法）
① 审题（30 秒）
读懂题目 → 判断题型（💬 观点类 / 🌐 现象类）→ 确定用哪套模板
② 填充（5 分钟）
定所属大类 → 从观点库挑 2-3 观点 → 选 1 数据 + 1 经验 → 按模板填空
③ 收尾（1 分钟）
语法检查 → 切题检查 → 字数检查（200-300 词）
💡 防模板痕迹
功能词换着用（causes→causal factors→drivers）；数据可编造但要合理（20%/30%）
📐 两套模板速览（✅必说）
💬 观点类（Agree / Opinion / 利大于弊？）
P1It is irrefutable that the concept of【核心词】is of paramount importance... From my perspective, I firmly believe the advantages are more compelling.
P2A principal benefit of【主题词】lies in its profound impact on【好处领域】. For instance, ...【数据】. Furthermore, from my own experience, ...【个人经验】.
P3Admittedly, we must be aware of【风险】. However, this stems from【根源】, not the concept itself. In fact, ...【方案】. Data from a 2024 report...【数据】.
P4In conclusion, I reaffirm my conviction that the advantages are far more significant.
🌐 现象类（Causes / Solutions / 正反影响？）
P1It is irrefutable that the phenomenon of【核心现象】has become a matter of public worry. This essay examines the【任务一】and【任务二】.
P2Regarding the【任务一】, a principal【cause/solution/effect】is arguably【核心点】. For instance, ...【数据】.
P3A further facet emerges when considering【任务二】. Data from a 2024 report indicate that...【数据】.
P4In culmination, the【任务一】and【任务二】I have articulated contribute to a deeper appreciation.
