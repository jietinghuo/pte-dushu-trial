A Missing Book · 丢失的书

#221056 · 道歉 Apology · 27.1秒

🎭 场景

你在宿舍/教室遇到了需要向同学或图书管理员道歉的情况，因为丢失的书。

🧠 答题思路

①谁=同学或图书管理员（宿舍/教室）；情境=丢失的书；目标=道歉+补救 → 这是 Apology 道歉。
②第1句直接道歉：Excuse me + I'm sorry for...，不狡辩。
③第2句Actually, + 简短原因（一两句带过）。
④第3句And also, + 补充句 I want to take responsibility... 表明承担态度。
⑤第4句So, + 具体补救（已做的/下一步）——内容分关键。
⑥第5句Anyway, 收尾 Thank you for your understanding. + 承诺。全程 30-40 秒。

📝 参考答案

  • 第1句 Hey, I'm really sorry—I think I might have lost the book you lent me last week.
  • 第2句 Actually, I looked everywhere in the dorm but couldn't find it.
  • 第3句 And also, I want to take responsibility for what happened, and I'm truly sorry about it.
  • 第4句 So, I'll buy you a new copy this weekend, or if you prefer, I can pay you back for it.
  • 收尾 Anyway, sorry again for the trouble, and I'll make sure this kind of thing never happens again.
