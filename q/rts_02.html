#221045 · 道歉 Apology · 24.6秒

🎭 场景

你在课堂/会议遇到了需要向教授或同学道歉的情况，因为演讲迟到。

🧠 答题思路

①谁=教授或同学（课堂/会议）；情境=演讲迟到；目标=道歉+补救 → 这是 Apology 道歉。
②第1句直接道歉：Excuse me + I'm sorry for...，不狡辩。
③第2句Actually, + 简短原因（一两句带过）。
④第3句And also, + 补充句 I want to take responsibility... 表明承担态度。
⑤第4句So, + 具体补救（已做的/下一步）——内容分关键。
⑥第5句Anyway, 收尾 Thank you for your understanding. + 承诺。全程 30-40 秒。

📝 参考答案

  • 第1句 Excuse me, professor.
  • 第2句 Actually, I'm sorry for being late to today's presentation.
  • 第3句 And also, I want to take responsibility for what happened, and I'm truly sorry about it.
  • 第4句 So, I have already prepared all my slides and I'm ready to present right now if it's okay.
  • 收尾 Anyway, thank you for your understanding, and I'll make sure this kind of thing never happens again.
