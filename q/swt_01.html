SWT 001 · 2014 Olympics（完整做题过程）

我们的题号：SWT-001　·　猩际题号：#1　·　标题：2014 Olympics　·　考过：517 次

题型：Summarize Written Text　·　目标：60-70 词单句 + 满分

做题方法：通读原文 → 找关键句（整句标注）→ 确定关系 → 选连接词 → 改写（每句改写方案+原因）→ 简单替换 → 标准答案 → 自评

📄 题目原文（完整文本）

11 February 2009 – Major athletic events around the globe – from the 2014 Sochi Olympics to an annual powerboat race in Norwegian fjords – are striving to neutralize their carbon footprint as part of a world-wide climate network, the United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP) said today.

The sporting events are the latest participants to join the network, and are particularly important for inspiring further global action on the environment, said Achim Steiner, UNEP Executive Director.

"It is, therefore, heartening that this passion for excellence increasingly encompasses environmental performance, with sporting organizations raising the bar ever higher at each and every tournament," he added.

Organizers of the 2014 Sochi Olympic and Paralympic Winter Games – to be held in a unique natural setting between the shores of the Black Sea and the soaring snow-capped Caucasus Mountains – say they will put an estimated $1.75 billion into energy conservation and renewable energy.

That investment will be dedicated to improving transport infrastructure and offsetting greenhouse gas emissions from the use of electricity, air travel and ground transportation, the reforestation of Sochi National Park and the development of green belts in the city.

🔍 第一步：找关键句（原文无删改 · 完整关键句标注）

扫读思路：2 分钟扫读。本文无转折词（无 however/but），重点用「首尾句 + 强调句 + 重复主题词」三招。主题词 carbon footprint / environment / network / energy 反复出现 → 主旨 = 体育赛事努力中和碳足迹。黄色高亮 = 选中的关键句（整句，无删改），括号内是选择原因：

第 1 段：11 February 2009 – Major athletic events around the globe – from the 2014 Sochi Olympics to an annual powerboat race in Norwegian fjords – are striving to neutralize their carbon footprint as part of a world-wide climate network, the United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP) said today.（整句为关键句 · 首段首句=总起主旨 · 含全部主题词 · ✅必选）

第 2 段：The sporting events are the latest participants to join the network, and are particularly important for inspiring further global action on the environment, said Achim Steiner, UNEP Executive Director.（整句为关键句 · 强调词 particularly important · 承接句1递进 · ✅必选）

第 3 段："It is, therefore, heartening that this passion for excellence increasingly encompasses environmental performance, with sporting organizations raising the bar ever higher at each and every tournament," he added.（引言评价 · 未选）

第 4 段：Organizers of the 2014 Sochi Olympic and Paralympic Winter Games – to be held in a unique natural setting between the shores of the Black Sea and the soaring snow-capped Caucasus Mountains – say they will put an estimated $1.75 billion into energy conservation and renewable energy.（整句为关键句 · 具体行动+数字考点 $1.75 billion · ✅必选）

第 5 段：That investment will be dedicated to improving transport infrastructure and offsetting greenhouse gas emissions from the use of electricity, air travel and ground transportation, the reforestation of Sochi National Park and the development of green belts in the city.（整句为关键句 · 尾段首句=投资用途 · ✅必选）

舍弃的句子及原因：第 3 段 Steiner 引言（个人评价，非事实，且引语改写成本高）。其余 4 句全部保留。

🔗 第二步：确定关系（关键句之间的逻辑）

  1. 句 1 → 句 2：并列/递进（句 1"赛事加入气候网络"，句 2"且意义重要"）→ 用 and 连接
  2. 句 2 → 句 3：对比（前面说意义，后面转向具体行动）→ 用 while 连接
  3. 句 3 → 句 4：承接/解释（句 3 投资 17.5 亿，句 4 说明用途）→ 用 with the funding... 介词短语挂接

✂️ 第四步：改写（关键句整句抄下 + 每句改写方案与原因）

为什么改写：4 个关键句整句合计约 100+ 词，远超 75 上限 → 必须压缩到 60-70；同时避免整句照抄（防判抄袭）。改写原则：删解释/引用/修饰（不删内容），保留主题词和数字。

关键句 1（整句抄下）：

"11 February 2009 – Major athletic events around the globe – from the 2014 Sochi Olympics to an annual powerboat race in Norwegian fjords – are striving to neutralize their carbon footprint as part of a world-wide climate network, the United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP) said today."

如何改写：① 删开头日期 "11 February 2009"；② 删两处破折号解释 "-- from the 2014 Sochi Olympics to an annual powerboat race in Norwegian fjords --"；③ 删引用框架 "the United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP) said today"；④ 保留主干 "Major athletic events around the globe are striving to neutralize their carbon footprint as part of a world-wide climate network"。

改写原因：日期非内容；破折号中间是解释说明（补充哪些赛事），删掉不损失主旨；引用框架（谁说的）不是答案内容；主干含全部主题词，必须保留。

关键句 2（整句抄下）：

"The sporting events are the latest participants to join the network, and are particularly important for inspiring further global action on the environment, said Achim Steiner, UNEP Executive Director."

如何改写：① 删引用框架 "said Achim Steiner, UNEP Executive Director"；② 整句保留 "The sporting events are the latest participants to join the network, and are particularly important for inspiring further global action on the environment"。

改写原因：引用框架（谁说的）非内容；主干含强调词 particularly important（招4），保留能拿满内容分。

关键句 3（整句抄下）：

"Organizers of the 2014 Sochi Olympic and Paralympic Winter Games – to be held in a unique natural setting between the shores of the Black Sea and the soaring snow-capped Caucasus Mountains – say they will put an estimated $1.75 billion into energy conservation and renewable energy."

如何改写：① 删破折号地点修饰 "-- to be held in a unique natural setting... Caucasus Mountains --"；② 保留主干 "Organizers of the 2014 Sochi Olympic and Paralympic Winter Games will put an estimated $1.75 billion into energy conservation and renewable energy"。

改写原因：破折号中间是地理位置解释，与主旨无关可删；数字 $1.75 billion 是考点必须保留。

关键句 4（整句抄下）：

"That investment will be dedicated to improving transport infrastructure and offsetting greenhouse gas emissions from the use of electricity, air travel and ground transportation, the reforestation of Sochi National Park and the development of green belts in the city."

如何改写：① 删长列举 "from the use of electricity, air travel and ground transportation, the reforestation of Sochi National Park and the development of green belts in the city"（只留核心 improving transport infrastructure and offsetting greenhouse gas emissions）；② "That investment will be dedicated to" 降级为介词短语 "with the funding dedicated to"。

改写原因：长列举细节占字数，删掉只保留核心（主题词 greenhouse gas emissions 仍在）；整句降级为 with 短语，省连接词且字数骤减。

✅ 第六步：标准答案（67 词 · 单句 · 满分）

说明：黄色高亮 = 相对关键句原文「改动的地方」（改动对照见上一步）；未高亮部分 = 沿用关键句用词：

Major athletic events around the globe are aiming to reduce their carbon footprint as part of a world-wide climate network, and the sporting events are particularly significant for inspiring further global action on the environment, while the organizers of the Sochi Olympics will invest an estimated $1.75 billion in energy conservation and renewable energy, with the funding dedicated to improving transport infrastructure and offsetting greenhouse gas emissions.

检查：首字母大写（Major）✓　单句 ✓（主句 + and + while + with 短语）　67 词（60-70 最佳区间）✓　语法正确 ✓　高亮 4 处 = 全部改动点，其余沿用关键句。
