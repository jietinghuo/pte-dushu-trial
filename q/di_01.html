DI #2 · 分类：图片图

📐 本题怎么答（图片图 · 带连接词模板）

✅必说｜开场定题：This image shows [主题].
✅必说｜分区域：In the top / bottom area, left / right side of the picture, we can see [内容].
✅必说｜中间区：In the center of the picture, there is / are [数量] [人物/物品].
➕选说｜主体细节：Besides, [人物/物品] is / are doing / wearing / holding [细节].
➕选说｜背景氛围：Meanwhile, in the background / foreground, [内容].
✅必说｜总结：Overall, this picture clearly shows [主题].

🔰 基础版答案（现场快说 · 30-39 秒）

现场快说版：只按模板说方位/步骤 + 物品 + 颜色/数量，不背功能从句；卡壳就补背景（颜色/阳光/草地）。

This image shows how to place your hands when typing. In the top area of the picture, we can see two labels, RIGHT and WRONG. On the left side, the correct hand is straight. On the right side, the wrong hand is bent. Besides, the correct way keeps the wrists straight and relaxed. Meanwhile, in the background, we can see a keyboard. Overall, this picture clearly shows the correct way to type.

In contrast, the wrong way shows bent wrists and awkward fingers, which is marked as WRONG. Overall, this picture clearly shows that keeping your hands straight while typing is the healthy way.
