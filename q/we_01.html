📄 题目原文

(1272)
With cities expanding, governments should look forward to creating better networks of public transport available for everyone rather than building more roads for vehicle owning population. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

① 审题 · 分析题目要求

【题干】(1272)
With cities expanding, governments should look forward to creating better networks of public transport available for everyone rather than building more roads for vehicle owning population. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

【审题】题目问：城市扩张时应优先建公共交通还是私人交通。属于观点类（比较两种方案），推荐【观点类】模板，立场=公共交通更优。

👉 推荐用【观点类】模板：本题属于【观点类】，推荐使用【观点类】模板。按模板三步填充法完成。

② 填充 · 从观点库挑内容（本页范文用的就是这些）

该题核心观点（范文 P2 用）：

· integrated public transport reduces congestion and pollution（一体化公共交通减少拥堵与污染）

· public transport serves everyone, including the elderly and low-income groups（公共交通服务所有人，老人与低收入群体）

· private cars offer flexibility but impose high social costs（私家车灵活但社会成本高）

好处角度（P2 具体好处）：

· lower traffic congestion and cleaner air in city centres（市中心交通更通畅、空气更干净）

· affordable and reliable mobility for all residents（全体居民负担得起、可靠的出行）

· reduced commuting time and stress（通勤时间与压力减少）

弊端角度（P3 让步用）：

· high initial cost of building metro and bus networks（建设地铁公交网络初始成本高）

· inconvenience during construction periods（建设期出行不便）

解决方案（P3 解决用）：

· investing in integrated public transport networks（投资一体化公共交通网络）

· incentivizing carpooling and low-emission vehicles（激励拼车与低排放车辆）

· phasing construction to reduce disruption（分期建设减少干扰）

可编造数据（P2/P3 各用 1 个）：

· a 30% reduction in traffic congestion（交通拥堵减少30%）

· a 25% decrease in air pollution levels（空气污染水平下降25%）

· a 20% increase in public transport usage（公共交通使用率提升20%）

个人经验（P2 加分）：

· my direct involvement in commuting by metro every day has tangibly enhanced my awareness of how reliable public transport saves time

📝 满分范文（标注版）

标注说明：黄色高亮 = 你填的自定义内容（观点 / 数据 / 个人经验），其余为模板固定句。每段后附中文翻译 + 填充理由。

P1 引言（亮立场）

It is irrefutable that the concept of public transport networks is of paramount importance in modern society. The assertion regarding how governments should plan transport in expanding cities has become a frequent topic of public discourse. From my perspective, while recognizing possible shortcomings, I firmly believe that the advantages offered by investing in public transport are more compelling.

📖 中文：不可否认，公共交通网络的概念在现代社会中至关重要。关于政府应如何规划扩张中城市的交通这一主张，已成为公众经常讨论的话题。在我看来，在承认可能存在的不足的同时，我坚信投资公共交通所带来的好处更有说服力。

💡 为什么这么填：P1 引言（模板句，仅换关键词）：【public transport networks】= 题目核心词；【how governments should plan transport】= 改写题目背景；【investing in public transport】= 亮出立场（公共交通优先）。

P2 好处段（观点①+数据+经验）

A principal benefit of well-planned public transport lies in its profound impact on reducing congestion and pollution. It is widely recognized that its application can foster substantial advancements in areas like cleaner air in city centres and affordable mobility for all residents. For instance, recent studies from the University of Cambridge revealed that expanding metro and bus networks can consistently lead to a 30% reduction in traffic congestion, which underscores its profound impact. Furthermore, drawing from my own experience, my direct involvement in commuting by metro every day has tangibly enhanced my awareness of how reliable public transport saves time. Therefore, the significance of this particular element cannot be overstated.

📖 中文：规划良好的公共交通的一个主要好处在于其对减少拥堵和污染的深远影响。人们普遍认为，它的应用能促进市中心空气更清洁、全体居民负担得起出行等领域的实质性进步。例如，剑桥大学的近期研究表明，扩建地铁和公交网络能持续使交通拥堵减少30%，这印证了其深远影响。此外，从我自身经验来看，我每天乘地铁通勤切实增强了我对可靠公共交通节省时间的认识。因此，这一要素的重要性不容小觑。

💡 为什么这么填：P2 好处段：观点①【well-planned public transport】来自观点库核心观点；两个具体好处【cleaner air】【affordable mobility】来自「好处角度」库；数据【a 30% reduction in traffic congestion】来自「可编造数据」库；个人经验【commuting by metro】来自「个人经验」库——全部对应②填充区。

P3 让步段（风险→根源→方案+数据）

Admittedly, we must be aware of potential dangers, such as the high initial cost of building new lines. However, this issue typically stems from short-term budgeting, not the concept itself. In fact, these drawbacks can be successfully dealt with through phased construction and public-private partnerships. Data from a 2024 report by the United States indicate that cities investing heavily in public transit effectively contribute to a 25% decrease in car dependency, thereby efficiently preventing gridlock and wasted commuting hours.

📖 中文：诚然，我们必须意识到潜在的风险，例如建设新线路的高昂初始成本。然而，这一问题通常源于短期预算安排，而非该概念本身。事实上，这些弊端可以通过分期建设和公私合作来有效解决。美国2024年的一份报告显示，大力投资公共交通的城市有效促使私家车依赖度下降25%，从而有效防止了交通堵塞和通勤时间浪费。

💡 为什么这么填：P3 让步段：风险【high initial cost】来自「弊端」库；根源【short-term budgeting】为逻辑补充；方案【phased construction + partnerships】来自「解决方案」库；数据【25% decrease in car dependency】来自「数据」库——让步段=承认缺点→指出根源→给出方案，全部贴题。

P4 结论（重申立场）

In conclusion, while admitting the aforementioned problems, I reaffirm my conviction that the advantages of public transport networks are far more significant. A collaborative strategy by all stakeholders is therefore crucial to managing this challenge.

📖 中文：总之，在承认上述问题的同时，我重申我的信念：公共交通网络的优势远为重要。因此，所有利益相关者采取协作策略对于应对这一挑战至关重要。

💡 为什么这么填：P4 结论（模板句）：重申立场【public transport networks are far more significant】与 P1 首尾呼应。

🧠 背诵思路（完善版）

· 【P2 记忆点】well-planned public transport = 核心观点；cleaner air + affordable mobility = 两个好处；30% congestion reduction = 数据；metro commuting = 个人经验
